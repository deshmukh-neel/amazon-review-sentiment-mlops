03 / MODEL CARD

Transparent by design.

Methodology

Word TF-IDF unigrams and bigrams feed a regularized logistic regression classifier.

Limitations

Binary English sentiment only. Sarcasm, mixed opinions, domain shift, and neutral reviews can reduce reliability.

Demonstration only

Predictions are for portfolio demonstration, not decisions about people, products, or businesses.
